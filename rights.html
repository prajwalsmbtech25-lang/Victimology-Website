KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

Victim Rights

Understanding the rights of victims is an important part of creating a fair, supportive and accessible justice system.

⚖️

Right to Justice

Victims should have access to fair and appropriate processes for seeking justice.

🛡️

Right to Protection

Victims may need protection from intimidation, retaliation and further harm.

ℹ️

Right to Information

Victims should be able to receive relevant information about procedures, services and support.

💰

Right to Compensation

Depending on applicable law, victims may have access to compensation or other forms of assistance.

🤝

Right to Support

Victims can benefit from appropriate social, legal and community support services.

🗣️

Right to Be Heard

Victims should have appropriate opportunities to express their concerns within the justice process.
