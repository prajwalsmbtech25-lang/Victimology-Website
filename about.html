ABOUT VICTIMOLOGY

Understanding the Victim's Role in Justice

Victimology is the study of victims, their experiences, needs, rights, and their relationship with the criminal justice system.

Understanding victimology helps create a justice system that protects victims, respects their rights, and provides appropriate support throughout the legal process.

This website provides information about different types of victims, victim rights, the justice process, the impact of victimization, and available support.
