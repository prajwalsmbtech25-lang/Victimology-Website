UNDERSTANDING VICTIMS

Types of Victims

Victims can experience crime and victimization in different ways. Understanding these categories helps us recognize their needs and provide appropriate support.

01

Primary Victims

Individuals who directly experience a crime or harmful act are considered primary victims.

02

Secondary Victims

Family members, friends or others who are affected by the victimization of another person.

03

Vulnerable Victims

People who may require additional protection and support because of their circumstances or vulnerability.

04

Repeat Victims

Individuals who experience victimization more than once over a period of time.

UNDERSTANDING THE EFFECTS

Impact of Victimization

Victimization can affect different areas of a person's life. Understanding these effects helps society provide appropriate support and assistance.

🧠

Psychological Impact

Victimization may affect emotional well-being and can lead to fear, stress, anxiety or difficulty feeling safe.

🩺

Physical Impact

Some forms of victimization can result in physical consequences and may require medical care or recovery.

👥

Social Impact

Victimization can affect relationships, education, employment and participation in community life.

💰

Financial Impact

Victims may experience financial difficulties because of losses, expenses or disruption to work and education.

Reporting the Incident

04

Resolution
