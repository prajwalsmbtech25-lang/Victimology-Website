UNDERSTANDING THE SYSTEM

Justice Process

The criminal justice process involves several important stages. Understanding these stages can help victims know what to expect and where support may be available.

01

Report the Crime

The incident may be reported to the appropriate authorities and the victim's complaint is formally recorded.

02

Investigation

Authorities investigate the incident, collect relevant information and examine available evidence.

03

Legal Proceedings

The case moves through the legal process, where evidence and statements may be considered by the appropriate court.

04

Resolution

The case reaches a legal outcome according to applicable laws and the judicial process.
